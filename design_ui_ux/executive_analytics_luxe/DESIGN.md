## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes high-end executive SaaS aesthetics with authoritative statistical governance. Designed specifically for high-level decision-makers and regional statistical leaders, the visual tone conveys institutional trust, precision, and modern technological sophistication. 

The aesthetic is characterized by:
- **Pristine Airy Canvas**: High-density metrics breathe freely within generous spacing, elevated on pure white cards over a faint crystalline navy-tinted canvas.
- **Modern Luxury Precision**: Deep maritime navy tones paired with warm gold accents replace clinical, cold enterprise grays, creating an elite executive cockpit.
- **Rigorous Data Integrity**: Statistical ambiguity is removed through distinct visual differentiation between positive achievement, lagging performance, and explicit missing data states.

## Colors

The palette establishes an executive hierarchy by pairing deep ocean navy with warm artisanal gold and cool slate accents.

### Palette Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary Navy (`#122347`, `#1A3263`)**: Used for anchor elements, high-contrast headings, primary CTA buttons, active sidebar pills, and focal chart line paths.
- **Secondary Slate (`#547792`, `#7A9AB5`)**: Drives structural clarity, subtle category tags, secondary line plots, and non-distracting icon strokes.
- **Warm Gold Accent (`#FFC570`)**: The jewel tone of the interface. Reserved for milestone achievements, peak radial arcs, glowing interactive focus points, and critical attention alerts.
- **Soft Cream & Canvas (`#EFD2B0`, `#F6F8FB`)**: Provides warm counter-balances to deep cool tones, used for soft pill containers, counter badges, and zebra row highlights.

### Strict Data Semantic Tokens
- **Tercapai (`#1E824C` on `#EAF8EE`)**: Denotes full or surpassing completion rates with a green success badge.
- **Di Bawah Target (`#B45309` on `#FFF7EC`)**: Signals moderate underperformance without alarming the user.
- **Kosong / Missing Data (`#C53030` on `#FEEEEE`)**: Explicitly indicates unsubmitted regional returns (`∅`), strictly isolated from numerical zero (`0`).

## Typography

Typography balances geometric structure with humanistic readability. 

- **Display & Headings (Outfit)**: Provides geometric authority and crisp visual impact across major KPI numerals and dashboard headlines. Numerical stats leverage tabular figure alignment (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during real-time data updates.
- **Body & Data Grid (Manrope)**: Delivers exceptional legibility within dense statistical tables, sublabels, and interactive widgets.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a flexible 12-column executive desktop grid that contracts seamlessly down to tablet and mobile single-column views.

### Structure
- **Persistent Left Navigation**: 260px fixed desktop sidebar collapsing to a 72px icon rail on tablet viewports.
- **Contextual Sticky Header**: 72px height containing the regional WITA live clock, search bar, and user profile.
- **Row 1 (KPI Metrics)**: 4-column balanced grid displaying critical indicators.
- **Row 2 (Hero Analytics vs. Operations)**: 8-column hero spline chart container paired with a 4-column operational widget stack (Weekly Peak Chart, Radial Progress Speedometer, AI Assistant Orb).
- **Row 3 (Regional Monitoring Table)**: 12-column full-width container with a frozen first column for geographic regions (Kabupaten/Kota).
- **Slide-Over Widget Drawer**: 420px fixed slide-over panel entering from the right viewport boundary, utilizing deep ambient drop shadows.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids flat gray drop shadows. Elevation is achieved through multi-layered ambient shadows tinted with the primary navy brand color (`rgba(26, 50, 99, ...)`), producing an authentic luminous depth over the `#F6F8FB` background canvas.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base (`#F6F8FB`)**: Flat foundational surface.
- **Resting Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Elevated with `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(26, 50, 99, 0.05)` and framed by an ultra-fine 1px border (`#EAEFF5`).
- **Interactive Card Hover**: Lifts smoothly to `0 10px 25px -4px rgba(26, 50, 99, 0.09)`.
- **Slide-Over Modal Drawer**: Elevated with deep directional occlusion: `-12px 0 35px -5px rgba(26, 50, 99, 0.12)`.
- **AI Glowing Orb & Gold Micro-Interactions**: Ambient accent luminance using dual-stage glow: `0 4px 18px rgba(255, 197, 112, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp statistical rigor with welcoming luxury curvature. 

- **Standard Cards & Modals**: Grounded at an 18px radius (approximated by token level `2`), providing a modern, smooth aesthetic.
- **Pills & Micro Tags**: Styled with full pill rounding (`9999px`) for quick visual scanning of percentage variations, active filters, and operational indicators.
- **Form Controls & Inputs**: Formatted with a clean 10px radius to retain an architectural, structured profile.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Deep navy fill (`#1A3263`) with white text, 10px radius, padding `0.75rem 1.25rem`, accompanied by a navy-tinted shadow (`0 4px 14px rgba(26, 50, 99, 0.22)`). Hovers to `#122347`.
- **Accent Highlight**: Warm gold fill (`#FFC570`) with `#0F1A30` text, paired with a gold ambient glow. Used exclusively for primary promotional triggers and AI suggestions.
- **Secondary Ghost**: Outlined with 1px `#EAEFF5`, white background, `#3E4B5E` body text, shifting to `#EDF2FA` on hover.

### KPI Stat Cards
- Enclosed in an 18px radius white card with an ultra-fine `#EAEFF5` border.
- Features a pastel square container (`44px × 44px`, 12px radius) housing a single-line icon in the top right.
- Numerical values display in `28px Outfit Bold` with tabular numbers.
- Sub-text pairs with a micro pill badge showing trend direction (`▲ +15.5%` in `#EAF8EE` or `▼ -4.2%` in `#FFF7EC`).

### Hero Spline Area Chart
- Pure white background card with an upper control header (Segment toggle and date-range picker).
- Gradient area fill descending from `#1A3263` at 20% opacity to `rgba(26, 50, 99, 0.0)`.
- Bottom 3-segment color breakdown bar visualizing performance share across domains: Sosial, Produksi, Nerwilis, and IPDS.

### Monitoring Data Table
- Frozen/sticky region column fixed on the left with a subtle vertical divider.
- Alternating zebra rows using `#FAF4ED` at 40% opacity.
- Cell values display raw targets alongside semantic achievement badges:
  - **Tercapai**: `105.0% ✓` with `#1E824C` text and `#EAF8EE` fill.
  - **Di Bawah Target**: `87.5% ⚠️` with `#B45309` text and `#FFF7EC` fill.
  - **Belum Ada Data**: `∅ Kosong` with `#C53030` text and `#FEEEEE` fill (visually distinct from `0`).

### Interactive Right-Side Widgets
- **Weekly Activity Bar Chart**: Minimal vertical column track in `#EDF2FA` with the current peak day highlighted in vibrant `#1A3263`.
- **Semi-Circular Radial Speedometer**: Progress arc rendered in warm gold (`#FFC570`) against an unfilled neutral track (`#EFD2B0`), with percentage value (`78.4%`) centered inside.
- **AI Assistant Card**: Minimal query input field beneath a centered 3D glossy orb rendering a navy-to-gold gradient with a radial glow.

### Slide-Over Widget Drawer
- 420px right-anchored panel with a dismiss header and active category pills (`#Performa`, `#Wilayah`, `#Jadwal`).
- Interactive card catalog items showcasing mini preview skeletons with a "+ Tambah" action button.
